## Findings

1. **The boundary lives at ~75–150%BW** depending on friction — the old blueprint's
   invented "15 N" (≈12%BW) figure would have been deep inside the safe region,
   exactly why projected numbers are banned.
2. **μ = 0.10 is a different regime:** the policy falls even at 10%BW (and shows
   non-monotonic seed noise) — on ice the policy can barely walk regardless of push.
   The walking-itself-fails boundary sits between μ 0.10 and 0.25.
3. **Non-monotonicity in friction at 100%BW:** μ 0.70 → 1/4 fell but μ 1.00 → 3/4
   fell — more grip is not always safer (plausibly trip-vs-slide). 4 seeds is far too
   few to call this real; the full 16-seed sweep will resolve it. Parked in NOTES.

## Grid-range recommendation (§14 #6 — needs Taylor sign-off)

- Friction: **μ ∈ [0.05, 1.00], 20 linear steps of 0.05** — walking-failure boundary
  inside the left portion; DR band U(0.4, 1.0) annotated on the axis; model default
  0.6 inside.
- Push: **10–200%BW, 20 linear steps of 10** — push boundary crosses mid-map at all
  frictions; both edges saturated (all-survive bottom rows at high μ, all-fall top).
- Both axes then have the boundary comfortably inside with margin on both sides; no
  edge-clipping re-run risk observed in pilot data.
